What changes, and why

  • Background stays #020E0A and water stays cyan #00E5FF, both from the locked palette. The live build drifted to #03120C and #00D9FF.
  • Inhalation purple lightens from #B366FF to #B488FF. The live purple measures 4.40:1 on a card, below the 4.5:1 that small text needs. The new one measures above 6:1.
  • Gold #C8A84B moves to the "Both routes today" badge. The live synergy yellow #FFD900 has no job after the bonus was removed.
  • Body text gets a near-white (#E8F3EF headline, #C9DDD7 body) instead of the live pale teal everywhere. Labels use a muted sage that still clears 6:1. The live app dims whole screens with opacity, which is where most of its unreadable text comes from.
  • Glow on headline numbers only. Titles and labels lose the glow.
  • Mint #02C39A stays with the wordmark and the link to gatewayh2.com, so the app and the site share one brand mark.

Suggested, not ruled · the ring scale

The full circle is 15.0 HQ, so the 10.0 reference tick sits two thirds of the way round. A full ring at 10.0 would read as a goal to fill, which R-365 rules out. On a day above 15.0 the scale steps up in fives and the tick moves with it. Tell me if you want a different scale.
