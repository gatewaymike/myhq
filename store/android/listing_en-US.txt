APP NAME (30 max)
MyHQ: Hydrogen Intake Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Log hydrogen water and inhalation. See your daily total in one unit, HQ™.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4,000 max)
MyHQ counts hydrogen water and hydrogen inhalation in one unit, the Hydrogen Quotient (HQ™), and shows the arithmetic behind every figure.

WHAT 1 HQ IS
1.0 HQ = 500 mL of water at 1.6 mg/L = 0.80 mg of H₂.
Water: HQ = (mL ÷ 500) × (mg/L ÷ 1.6)
Inhalation: HQ = (minutes ÷ 30) × (hydrogen flow in mL/min ÷ 300) × 2, with flow taken at the machine outlet.
The same public formula applies to every device, whoever makes it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Set up your equipment once: water concentration in mg/L, or hydrogen flow in mL/min
• Log the water you drank or an inhalation session in a few taps, or use the session timer
• See today's total against a 10.0 HQ daily reference, a value inside a range that is neither a floor nor a ceiling
• Tap "Show the math" to see exactly how each number was calculated
• Review your history and 14-day or 30-day trends by route
• Switch between English and Traditional Chinese at any time

YOUR DATA

WHAT MYHQ IS NOT
MyHQ records intake figures only. HQ is not a medical device or medical advice. Consult your doctor before making medical decisions.
